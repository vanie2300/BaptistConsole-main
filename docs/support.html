Support Baptist Console

Baptist Console is a ministry-driven, open-source project (MIT licensed) created to serve churches — developed under the leadership of Malabuhan Bible Baptist Church, Pastor Regino Kapunan.

Free forever. There is no subscription, no license fee, and no premium tier. Ministry support keeps the project alive.
