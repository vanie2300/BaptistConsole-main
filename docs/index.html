Everything a projection booth needs

Two presenters, one console, built for the reality of a Sunday morning.

Bible Presenter

The full KJV 1611 text with book, chapter and verse browsing. Jump straight to a reference like John 3:16, or search by keyword across the whole Bible or the current book, with results highlighted.

Hymn Presenter

Keep your own hymn library, search it, add to it, or trim it down — with a live next-slide preview so you always know what's coming up.

Multi-display projection

Send either presenter to any connected screen, filling it edge to edge. A pulsing Live light in the console shows exactly when a projection is open.

Full presentation control

Set colors, fonts and background images per module, toggle references on or off, and preview every change before it reaches the screen. Press Esc to close all presenters at once.

Auto-fitting text

Verses and hymn lyrics resize to fill the screen automatically, with a readable floor so nothing shrinks past the back pew.
