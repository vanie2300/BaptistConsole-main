Capabilities

Everything you need for clear, distraction-free projection

Bible Presenter

Full KJV 1611 text, reference and keyword search, and verse projection with auto-fit text.

Hymn Presenter

Your own hymn library with live next-slide preview and auto-fitting lyrics that stay readable.

Multi-display projection

Send presentations to any connected display — project to several screens at once.

Full presentation control

Colors, fonts, and backgrounds to match your space. Esc dismisses, settings save live.
