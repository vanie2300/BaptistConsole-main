Welcome to
Baptist Console
Church Presentation Suite — a few quick steps to get presenting.
  • 1Pick a verse. Type a reference like John 3:16 or search a word in the Bible tab.
  • 2Project it. Press Present and choose which display opens the projection.
  • 3Present hymns. Open the Hymns tab, pick a hymn, and press Present. Arrow keys move between slides, Esc closes.
  • 4Switch freely. Presenter windows are normal windows — Alt+Tab away any time.
  • 5Make it yours. Settings → theme, colors, fonts, and separate Bible/Hymn background images.
